About Me
Platform ops, community first.

I spent 3+ years inside platforms — not as a user, but as someone who reviewed, flagged, and tracked content at scale. As part of a Risk Inspection team on a major social media platform, I built keyword sets to detect policy-violating content and developed a practical understanding of how format, tone, and content signals affect what reaches which audiences.

That inside-out perspective is what makes me approach content differently. I understand why certain posts surface to certain communities — and just as importantly, why others get ignored or feel out of place. Writing that fits a community isn't about sounding friendly; it's about understanding the unwritten rules of how people talk in that space.

More recently, at a language center, I ran the full content cycle: writing Facebook posts and sales scripts tailored to specific audience segments, seeding communities, following up leads via Zalo and Messenger, and independently building tracking sheets, checklists, and reporting templates from scratch. I'm comfortable working remotely and communicating in English for both written content and team meetings.
